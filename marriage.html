You are each other’s.

Seven Wednesday evenings at our home in Wainui, a fortnight apart. Each one is a short film over supper, then time for the two of you to talk.

How the evenings work

Just the two of you

Every conversation is between the two of you, at your own table.

A journal each

Yours to keep, with the main ideas and the questions for each evening.
