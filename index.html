You are wondering what we would work on.

You are welcome to start anywhere.

I am Brendan Vorster, a registered psychologist. I work with people through coaching and counselling, whichever fits where you actually are. My way of working is solution-oriented. If what you need is clinically directed treatment, I will say that early and help you find it. Online across New Zealand, or in person in Auckland by arrangement.

For you

Identity and purpose, career changes, burnout and wellbeing, confidence, resilience, and the decisions that keep circling back without resolving.

For leaders and teams

Executive and leadership coaching, team development, and work on how a system is actually operating: roles, authority, boundaries, and the task everyone assumes everyone else understands. Useful when the problem is not really one person.

For health and wellbeing

The psychological side of living with a health condition or changing a health behaviour: motivation, adherence, habits, sleep, pain, and the adjustment that follows a diagnosis.

This works alongside your medical care, never instead of it. I do not diagnose physical conditions, prescribe diets or exercise, or advise on medication.

You are not starting from nothing.

We start with a conversation

No forms and no commitment. Send a short note about what is bringing you here. You do not need to have it worked out first.

Season before direction

Some seasons call for movement and a clear goal. Others call for steadiness and rest before anything else is asked of you. Reading which one you are in comes before choosing what we do.

You know more than you think

Much of what is useful is already somewhere in your own account of things. My job is to draw it out and help you see the pattern, rather than hand you my answer and hope it fits.

You are not meant to carry it alone

Two questions sit underneath the work: why are you doing this, and who is helping you carry it. And if I am not the right person for what you need, I will say so and help you find who is.

I have spent more than a decade working with people and with organisations, which means a conversation here can hold both the person in front of me and the system they work inside. I am a registered psychologist, and I keep my practice under professional supervision.

My own working conviction, for what it is worth: the relationship does more of the work than any method does. Methods still matter, and I use ones with evidence behind them, but they sit second.
