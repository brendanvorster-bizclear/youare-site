I'm Brendan Vorster, a registered psychologist. I work with people through coaching, counselling and therapy — whichever meets you where you are.

It's psychology brought to real life: working with the deeper drivers beneath what you do, not just the surface. The work is built around identity and change rather than problems alone, and the aim is simple — to help you understand what's really holding you back, and move forward with clarity, purpose and confidence.
