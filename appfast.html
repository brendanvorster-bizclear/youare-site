Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Overview

This policy explains what App Fast ("the app") stores and how it's used. App Fast is made by You Are Ltd (formerly Bizclear Limited), a New Zealand company ("we", "us").

What App Fast stores on your phone

App Fast keeps a small amount of information, all of it on your device:

  • App selections: the apps you choose to fast from, the fast you set for each, and their fasting hours.
  • Stats: your holds, breaks and fasting streaks.
  • Encouraged app logs: time you choose to log in apps you want more time in.
  • Settings: your preferences, including the words you put on your shields.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect, view or transmit any content from your apps
  • We don't read your messages, emails or browsing history
  • We don't track your location
  • We don't collect information that identifies you
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools
  • We don't share data with third parties
  • We don't show advertisements

Purchases

Free trials and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Screen Time

App Fast uses Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) only to manage shields on your device. Within Apple's privacy framework the app receives anonymous tokens for the apps you select, so it doesn't learn which apps they are. All shield management happens on your device.

Data storage

Everything is stored on your device using standard iOS storage (UserDefaults and the app's shared container). Nothing is sent to a server. Removing App Fast removes this data.

Children

App Fast isn't designed for or directed at children under 13. It's for people managing their own screen time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Common questions

My apps aren't being shielded

App Fast needs Screen Time authorisation to place shields. If shields stop working:

  • Open Settings → Screen Time and check Screen Time is on
  • Open App Fast and check your apps are still selected
  • iOS sometimes clears selections after an update, so choose your apps again if the list is empty
  • Restart your iPhone and try again

I can't get into an app on a Full Fast

That's how a Full Fast works. To open the app, change it to a Lighter Fast or adjust its fasting hours in App Fast.

How do I change which apps are shielded?

Open App Fast and change your apps, their fast or their hours. Changes apply straight away.

Do you collect my data?

No. App Fast runs on your phone and sends nothing to us. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Subscriptions and codes

App Fast is free for 14 days, then monthly or every six months, billed by Apple. To manage or cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can redeem a code inside App Fast.
