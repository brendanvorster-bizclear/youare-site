YOU ARE LIMITED, trading as You Are

This explains what I hold about you, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the Health Information Privacy Code 2020 and the Privacy Act 2020.

What I collect

Your name and contact details. What you tell me about why you are here and what we work on. Brief notes from our sessions. Payment records. Where relevant, the name of your GP or another professional involved in your care.

I only collect what I need to do the work properly.

If you save a card, the number goes directly to Stripe, the payment provider, and is stored there. I see the last four digits and the card type, which is enough to know which card it is.

Why I collect it

To provide the coaching and counselling you have asked for, to keep track of our work so that each session builds on the last, to meet my professional and legal obligations, and to invoice you.

Where it is kept

Your information is held in a secure practice management system with access limited to me, and in secure email.

The system is Zanda. For New Zealand practices it holds information in Amazon Web Services data centres in the Sydney region, encrypted in storage and in transit. Information held outside New Zealand is protected by privacy safeguards comparable to those required here.

Paper notes, where I make any, are stored securely and destroyed once transferred.

Who can see it

Only me, except in these circumstances.

  • Serious risk. If I believe there is a serious and imminent risk to your safety or someone else’s, I may need to share relevant information. Wherever possible I will talk with you first.
  • Legal requirement. If a court or the law requires disclosure.
  • With your permission. If you ask me to share information with your GP, another professional, or anyone else, I will do so only with your agreement.

If your sessions are funded by an employer or an Employee Assistance Programme, they will normally receive confirmation that sessions took place for invoicing. They do not receive the content of what we discuss.

How long I keep it

Health information must be kept for ten years from the date I last provided you with a service. After that it is securely destroyed.

A saved card is removed when we finish, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you. You can ask me to correct anything you believe is wrong, and if I do not agree to change it, you can ask that your view is recorded alongside it.

Ask me and I will explain how. There is normally no charge.

If you have a concern

Talk to me first if you can.

If you would rather not, or it is not resolved, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (0800 803 909, privacy.org.nz), the Health and Disability Commissioner (0800 11 22 33, hdc.org.nz), or the New Zealand Psychologists Board (psychologistsboard.org.nz).
